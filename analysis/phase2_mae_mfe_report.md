## What Are MAE and MFE?

- **MAE (Maximum Adverse Excursion):** The worst drawdown from entry before exit
- **MFE (Maximum Favorable Excursion):** The best profit from entry before exit

**Why This Matters:**
- MAE tells us if stops are too tight (getting stopped out by noise) or too wide (giving up too much)
- MFE tells us if targets are too tight (exiting too early) or if we're capturing available profit
- MFE/MAE ratio indicates strategy quality (>1.5 is good, >2.0 is excellent)
