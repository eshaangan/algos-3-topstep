3. KEY INDICATORS OF OVERFITTING:
   ✓ Training accuracy >> Validation accuracy (>5% gap)
   ✓ Training ROC-AUC >> Validation ROC-AUC (>0.05 gap)
   ✓ Training win rate >> Validation win rate (>5% gap)
   ✓ Training profit factor >> Validation profit factor (>0.3 gap)
   ✓ High training accuracy but poor generalization
   ✓ Model performs well on training data but poorly on new data
